## How to Book

1. **Request a spot** using the [booking form](/#contact). Tell us your child's age, the class you'd like and any allergies.
2. **We confirm** your spot within 24–48 hours.
3. **Your child cooks!** Stay and watch your young chef in action. Class ends at noon.

**Cancellations:** Cancel at least 48 hours before class for a full refund or a make-up class.

## Allergy-friendly Promise

Your child's safety matters most.

- Tell us about any allergies when you book, and at least 48 hours before class, so we can adapt the recipe.
- Many recipes include nut-free, gluten-free and dairy-free options.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do parents stay or drop off?
Parents stay for the whole class.

### What should my child bring or wear?
Comfortable clothes that can get a little messy, closed-toe shoes and long hair tied back.

### Do kids take food home?
Yes! Kids eat together at the end of class and take home leftovers, the recipe card and their activity sheet.

### My child has a severe allergy. Can they join?
In most cases, yes. Contact us before booking so we can talk through the menu and how we prepare it. See our [Allergy-friendly Promise](#allergy-friendly-promise).

### Can siblings of different ages attend together?
Yes, kids aged 5–12 cook side by side. Older kids take on trickier tasks, while younger ones measure, mix and decorate.

### What happens if we miss a class?
Cancel at least 48 hours before class and you can choose a full refund or a make-up class.

### Can we pay with homeschool charter funds?
Yes! We are an approved vendor for **Mission Vista Academy**: request a purchase order from your school to pay for classes. See our [Homeschool Hub](/homeschool/) for more.
