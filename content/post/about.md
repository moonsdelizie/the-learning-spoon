Each class blends cooking skills with lessons in:
- 🌍 **Geography & Culture** - Discover the origins of foods and the traditions behind them.
- 🗣 **Languages** - Learn simple words and phrases connected to the recipe's culture.
- 👩‍🍳 **Teamwork & Life Skills** - Practice patience, turn-taking, and problem-solving.    
- 📐 **Core Subjects** - Math (measurements), Science (reactions), and Social Studies woven into every class.

## Homeschool Hub

The Learning Spoon Academy is built for homeschooling families:
- Classes count toward Science, Math, Geography, and Cultural Studies.
- Take-home recipe cards and worksheets extend learning beyond class.
- Flexible weekday scheduling makes it easy to fit into your homeschool rhythm.
