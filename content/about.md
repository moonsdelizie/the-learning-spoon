Every recipe tells a story about a place, its people and its traditions, and kids learn that story with their hands.

## Meet Your Teacher

Hi, I'm **Amani**, the founder of The Learning Spoon Academy and of [Moon's Delizie](https://www.instagram.com/moonsdelizie/). I love recipes from all over the world, and I believe the table is where diversity and unity meet. You can learn so much about a culture by exploring its dishes: the ingredients people grow, the spices they treasure and the way they gather to share a meal.

The more I cook, the more I notice how much we share. The same simple dishes, like flatbreads, dumplings and rice cooked with spices, appear in kitchens around the world under different names. After all, we are not that different.

Food is also how I explore emotions. Cooking together builds patience and confidence, and gives kids a natural way to talk about how they feel. I explore the same themes in books: I'm the [author of children's books](https://pluginpublications.dusigroup.com/authors/amani-aseel/) about emotional wellbeing, confidence and diversity, including *Frybread and Date* and *The Story Inside My Lunchbox*. At home, you'll often find me baking focaccia with my daughters.

## What Kids Learn

Each class blends cooking skills with lessons in:

- 🌍 **Geography & Culture**: discover where foods come from and the traditions behind them.
- 🗣 **Languages**: learn simple words and phrases connected to the recipe's culture.
- 👩‍🍳 **Teamwork & Life Skills**: practice patience, turn-taking and problem-solving.
- 📐 **Core Subjects**: math (measuring), science (what happens when we mix, heat and bake) and social studies, woven into every class.
