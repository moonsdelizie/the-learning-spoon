The Learning Spoon Academy is built for homeschooling families: every recipe becomes a hands-on lesson, and your child comes home with worksheets that keep the learning going.

## Subjects in Every Class

| Subject | What it looks like in the kitchen |
| -------- | -------- |
| **Math** | Measuring, fractions, doubling and halving a recipe |
| **Science** | Why dough rises, how heat changes food, mixing and dissolving |
| **Geography** | Finding the recipe's country on the map, climate and local ingredients |
| **Social Studies & Culture** | Traditions, holidays and how people eat around the world |
| **Language** | Simple words and phrases from the recipe's culture |
| **Life Skills** | Kitchen safety, teamwork, following instructions, cleaning up |

Classes can be logged toward Science, Math, Geography and Cultural Studies in your homeschool records.

## Take-home Materials

Every child takes home a **recipe card** and a **cultural activity sheet** to keep learning beyond class.
